Tmux Desk 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 12 日

Tmux Desk 是一个运行在你本地电脑上的 tmux 图形终端前端。我们的原则很简单：Tmux Desk 不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。

一句话总结：所有数据都留在你自己的设备上。应用不含任何遥测、分析、广告或账号系统，也不会把你的终端内容发往任何服务器。

我们不收集的数据

  • 不收集个人身份信息（姓名、邮箱、账号等）——应用根本没有登录或账号功能。
  • 不收集、不记录你在终端中输入或看到的任何内容、命令或输出。
  • 不含分析 / 遥测 / 崩溃上报 SDK，不做用户行为追踪。
  • 不含广告，也不与任何第三方共享数据。

数据存储在哪里

  • 本地偏好设置（窗口大小、页签顺序、语言等）仅保存在你电脑的应用配置目录中，从不离开设备。
  • 终端会话由你系统中的 tmux 管理，Tmux Desk 只是它的图形界面；会话内容属于 tmux 与你的 shell，不由本应用采集或传输。

网络访问

  • 桌面端界面通过内容安全策略（CSP connect-src 'none'）禁止对外发起任何网络请求。
  • 可选的「手机网页端」功能会在你主动开启时，于你自己的局域网内启动一个本地 WebSocket 服务，仅供你自己的设备访问；数据不经过任何我们的或第三方的服务器。

你的控制权

由于数据都在本地，删除应用及其本地配置目录即可清除全部相关数据。

联系方式

有任何隐私相关问题，可在项目仓库提交 issue：github.com/HeavenC/Tmux-Desk。

English

Tmux Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Tmux Desk is a graphical tmux terminal front-end that runs entirely on your local computer. Our principle is simple: Tmux Desk does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data.

In one sentence: everything stays on your own device. The app contains no telemetry, analytics, ads, or accounts, and it never sends your terminal contents to any server.

Data we do NOT collect

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, account) — the app has no login or account system at all.
  • No collection or logging of anything you type or see in the terminal — commands, output, or otherwise.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDKs; no behavioral tracking.
  • No advertising, and no data shared with any third party.

Where data lives

  • Local preferences (window size, tab order, language, etc.) are stored only in the app's config directory on your computer and never leave your device.
  • Terminal sessions are managed by tmux on your own system; Tmux Desk is only its GUI. Session contents belong to tmux and your shell and are never captured or transmitted by this app.

Network access

  • The desktop UI forbids all outbound network requests via a Content Security Policy (connect-src 'none').
  • The optional "phone web client" feature, only when you explicitly enable it, starts a local WebSocket server on your own LAN for access by your own devices. No data passes through any server operated by us or any third party.

Your control

Because all data is local, deleting the app and its local config directory removes all associated data.

Contact

For any privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository: github.com/HeavenC/Tmux-Desk.
